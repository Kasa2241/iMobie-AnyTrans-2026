## 🔌 Connecting your device

The software requires a connection to your iPhone to work.

1. Find your original Apple USB cable.
2. Plug one end into your iPhone.
3. Plug the other end into a USB port on your Windows computer.
4. Unlock your iPhone screen.
5. Tap Trust on your iPhone screen if a prompt appears.
6. Enter your iPhone passcode to confirm the connection.
7. Open the AnyTrans program from your desktop icon or the start menu.
8. Wait a few seconds for the software to identify your device.

## 📂 Managing your data

Once the software recognizes your device, you can start managing your content.

### Moving photos
You can move pictures from your phone to your computer. Click the Photo tab on the left sidebar. Select the photos you want to save. Choose the To PC button. Select a folder on your computer to store your images. Click Open to start the transfer.

### Backing up music
To move music, select the Music tab. You can view all songs currently on your device. Highlight the tracks you wish to move. Select the Export button to save them to your Windows drive. This helps you keep a safe copy of your songs.

### Sending messages
You can archive text messages for your records. Click the Messages icon. Wait for the software to load your chat history. Select the conversations you want to keep. Click the Export button to save these items as a text or PDF document.

## ❓ Frequently asked questions

Do I need iTunes installed to make this work?
No, this software works independently of iTunes. It handles the transfer of files on its own.

Can I move files from my computer to my phone?
Yes. Use the import function within any category tab to drag files from your computer folders into your iPhone.

Is my data safe during the transfer?
The software performs local transfers between your devices. It does not send your personal data to public cloud servers.

What should I do if the software does not see my iPhone?
Check that your USB cable works. Try a different USB port on your computer. Make sure you tapped Trust on your iPhone screen when you first connected it.

How do I check for updates?
Open the settings menu within the application. Select Check for Updates to see if a newer version exists.
